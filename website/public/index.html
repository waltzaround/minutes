What it does

Everything a good note-taker does, done privately.

Records both sides

Your microphone and the meeting audio are captured as separate tracks. It works with Zoom, Meet or Teams, and there's no bot joining the call.

Live transcript

NVIDIA Parakeet transcribes as you talk, with word-level timings, in 25 European languages. Any gaps are filled in when the meeting ends.

Knows who's talking

Nemotron speaker separation splits the conversation by voice. Enrol a colleague once and Minutes recognises them by name in later meetings.

Notes with receipts

A local language model drafts a summary, decisions, open questions and action items. Each point links to the moment in the transcript it came from.

Search everything

Press ⌘K on Mac or Ctrl+K on Windows to find any meeting, decision or sentence anyone said, and jump straight to that point in the transcript.

Notion & Linear, on your terms

Send notes to Notion and action items to Linear. You review every page and issue first, and nothing is written until you approve it.

How it works

Press record. Get notes.

Record

Start with ⇧⌘R on Mac or Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows. Pick your microphone and whether to capture the call audio.

Transcribe

Speech turns into text as you talk, and the transcript is finalised when you stop.

Attribute

Speakers are separated and matched to the people you've enrolled.

Summarise

The local model writes the notes, then you edit, search, export or sync them.

Privacy

Your meetings are none of our business.

Minutes has no servers to send your audio to. Speech recognition, speaker separation and the language model all run on your own hardware. Once you've downloaded the models, it works with Wi-Fi turned off.

  • Audio, transcripts and notes stay in a local database
  • Voice profiles are encrypted, and the key is kept in your system keychain
  • No accounts, no telemetry, no paid AI APIs
  • Audio is deleted after processing by default, or you can choose to keep it
  • Integrations only ever send what you approve

FAQ

Questions

Does anything get uploaded?

No. The only network requests are to download models, and to Notion or Linear if you connect them and approve a sync.

Do I need a bot in the call?

No. Minutes captures your computer's audio output directly, so it works with any meeting app, and in the room with just the microphone.

How accurate is it?

Parakeet TDT v3 is one of the most accurate open speech models. Every note links to the transcript it came from, so you can check it, and you can edit anything.

Should I tell people I'm recording?

Yes. Minutes reminds you to let everyone know before you start. Recording laws vary, so check what applies where you are.

macOS says it can’t verify the app. What do I do?

Preview builds aren’t signed or notarised yet. Drag Minutes to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open, and confirm in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Minutes.app in Terminal. Older builds are on GitHub.

What should I expect when installing on Windows?

Download the x64 installer above. This preview is unsigned, so Windows may show a publisher warning. It installs for your Windows user account and downloads WebView2 if needed. The AI models are downloaded during setup.

What does it cost?

Nothing to run. There are no subscriptions or usage fees, because all of the AI runs on your own hardware.
